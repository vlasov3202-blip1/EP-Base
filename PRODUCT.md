# EINEIRO product context

EINEIRO is a universal commerce and operations platform in active development. `EP-Base` is its historical repository name; automotive is a domain module.

Authoritative product decisions live in [EINEIRO_CANONICAL.md](EINEIRO_CANONICAL.md) and [the Silent Run addendum](EINEIRO_SILENT_RUN_PLATFORM_ACQUISITION_ADDENDUM.md). The current user task governs scope. Verify capabilities against the current checkout and relevant checks; dated development reports may include changes that are not committed.

- Market serves buyers expressing intent through camera, voice and scene objects. Preserve Camera Safe Zone, independent catalogs and replace-in-place anchors. Limited eligible Showcase placements do not replace organic relevance.
- Business serves owners and operational teams. Its purpose is to complete work and surface exceptions needing owner authority.
- Admin is the separate platform Control Plane. Do not mix platform authority with company-owner permissions.
- Universal Core separates Product, Offer, SKU and Inventory. Sale commission is 0%; services and external costs remain explicit.
- Current UI uses Russian text, vanilla JavaScript modules and CSS. Phone, tablet and desktop need their own usable compositions, keyboard support and reduced motion.
- Existing code contains demo/local state. Do not turn it into claims of live results, provider readiness or achieved business metrics.

This context records product constraints; it does not select a new visual identity. Inspect incumbent screens and the current brief before a design change.
